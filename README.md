## Case Study: Veltrix Digital Wallet

**Organisation:** Veltrix is a simulated fintech company in Nigeria offering a mobile digital wallet. Customers use it to store money, send money to other users, pay merchants, and view their transaction history.

**Users of the database:** Customers, merchants, customer support staff, fraud and compliance analysts, and system administrators.

**Major data stored:** Customer details, wallets and balances, transactions (deposits, withdrawals, transfers, payments), merchants, login devices, and audit logs.

**Key operations:** Register customers and open wallets, deposit and withdraw money, transfer funds between wallets, pay merchants, reverse failed transactions, generate statements, and flag suspicious activity.
